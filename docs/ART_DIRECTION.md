# Art Direction

## Overall Direction

Premium stylised-natural 3D.

The game should look rich and atmospheric without attempting
photorealism.

## Visual Priorities

1. Strong composition.
2. Attractive lighting.
3. Clear player silhouette.
4. Environmental depth.
5. Cohesive materials.
6. Environmental movement.
7. High-quality animation.

## Environment Layers

Scenes should make deliberate use of:

Foreground
- Nearby foliage
- Framing elements
- Occasional depth-of-field treatment

Gameplay plane
- Player
- Platforms
- Hazards
- Interactive objects

Midground
- Trees
- Rocks
- Water
- Structures

Background
- Forest
- Mountains
- Large scenery
- Sky
- Atmospheric fog

## Reference Images

Images stored under `docs/reference/` are visual references.

They are aspirational concept targets and should not automatically be
interpreted as technically achievable real-time renders.

Visual references define mood, composition and artistic direction more
than exact geometry or rendering complexity.

## Assets

Free and CC0 assets should be investigated before purchasing assets.

Third-party assets should be modified and integrated into a coherent
visual language rather than producing an obvious collection of
unrelated asset packs.

Important bespoke assets, particularly the protagonist, may justify
greater development effort.
