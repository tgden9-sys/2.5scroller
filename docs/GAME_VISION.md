# Game Vision

## Concept

2.5scroller is intended to be a proper polished game rather than a
small educational game.

The protagonist is a stylised 3D owl derived from the visual identity
of Apps4Owls.

The game combines responsive side-scrolling platform movement with
3D environments, cinematic presentation and gliding-based traversal.

## Experience

The player should enjoy simply controlling the owl.

Movement should feel responsive, expressive and polished before
additional gameplay systems are layered on top.

Expected core traversal includes:

- Run
- Jump
- Air control
- Wing-assisted movement
- Glide
- Dive
- Landing
- Environmental traversal

Additional abilities will be decided through prototyping.

## Presentation

The world is fully 3D but gameplay is primarily constrained to a
side-scrolling path.

The camera may use perspective and controlled movement to reveal depth,
curves in the route and cinematic environments.

The player does not have free control of the gameplay camera.

## Visual ambition

The game should have a premium stylised-natural appearance.

Initial visual direction:

- Lush woodland
- Moss-covered logs and rocks
- Layered vegetation
- Water
- Waterfalls
- Atmospheric depth
- Warm natural lighting
- Distant landscapes
- Moving foliage
- Environmental particles
- Strong foreground/midground/background separation

The goal is not photorealism.

Quality should come from art direction, composition, lighting,
materials, animation and environmental movement.

## Character

The owl should be recognisably related to the Apps4Owls mascot but
redesigned as a high-quality game character.

Priorities:

- Expressive eyes and face
- Strong silhouette
- Readable at gameplay distance
- Excellent animation
- Wings capable of believable gliding
- Personality in idle and movement animation

## Platform

Primary delivery is desktop web browser.

Web is a delivery method rather than an indication that the game must
target extremely low-end hardware.

Primary target:

1920x1080 at approximately 60 FPS on reasonably capable modern desktop
hardware.

## Development philosophy

Build a small vertical slice to near-release quality before attempting
a large game.

The first major proof should demonstrate:

1. Excellent player movement.
2. Strong camera behaviour.
3. A convincing owl character.
4. A beautiful small forest environment.
5. Successful browser delivery.
